# My Gift to You

Breathe in the smell of those flowers, deeply until it tastes.
Blow out the candle, this single flame,  ever so slowly. Watch it flicker and flicker until it finally dies. Only to reignite a fraction later. Undisturbed.
Again.
Smell the earthy lavender with each deep filling of your chest. Strain against your parting breath, unyieldingly fighting this single flame. _Watch_ it intimately.
Now - Let walls emerge from the grounds around you - brick, stone, mud, steel, trees. It will not matter. They are impenetrable all the same.
And now, we are here.
Alone. Safe. _Breathe._
The candle, will always be here. Those walls, will always be yours. My gift to you - safety. Calm. A place to _be_. A place without pain.
Walk. In either direction, as it is yours to explore. Yours to keep. Yours to maintain, destroy and rebuild.
