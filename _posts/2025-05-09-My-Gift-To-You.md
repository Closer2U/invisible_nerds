# My Gift to You

Breathe in the smell of those flowers, deeply until it tastes. <br>
Blow out the candle, this single flame,  ever so slowly. Watch it flicker and flicker until it finally dies. Only to reignite a fraction later. Undisturbed. <br>
Again. <br>
Smell the earthy lavender with each deep filling of your chest. Strain against your parting breath, unyieldingly fighting this single flame. _Watch_ it intimately.<br>
Now - Let walls emerge from the grounds around you - brick, stone, mud, steel, trees. It will not matter. They are impenetrable all the same. <br>
And now, we are here. <br>
Alone. Safe. _Breathe._ <br>
The candle, will always be here. Those walls, will always be yours. My gift to you - safety. Calm. A place to _be_. A place without pain. <br>
Walk. In either direction, as it is yours to explore. Yours to keep. Yours to maintain, destroy and rebuild.
